Brick and Masonry Work Built for Canadian Winters

Ontario winters are brutal on masonry. The repeated freeze-thaw cycle every season puts enormous pressure on mortar joints, brick faces, and concrete slabs. Inferior workmanship or the wrong materials will fail within just a few seasons, leaving homeowners paying twice for the same repair.

As specialist brick and masonry contractors in Scarborough, we use only weather-appropriate mortars, sealants, and concrete mixes rated for our climate. Every job is done with the long-term durability of your property in mind, not just how it looks on the day it is finished.
